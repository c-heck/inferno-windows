What the installer does:
  * Copies the driver (inferno_asio.dll), control panel and helper scripts to Program Files.
  * Registers "Inferno ARM ASIO" as an ASIO device (64-bit HKLM). No kernel driver,
    test signing or reboot is needed. A previous registration of a development build is
    replaced by the installed copy.
  * Optionally adds Windows Firewall rules and tunes the Dante network adapter.

Requirements and notes:
  * Windows 11 on Arm (e.g. Snapdragon X Elite). Choose the ARM64EC build for
    Nuendo / Cubase on Windows on Arm.
  * A wired Ethernet adapter on the Dante network. Select its address in the control
    panel after installation.
  * Inferno is a PTP follower only; another Dante device must be the clock leader.
  * Close your DAW before installing, updating or uninstalling.
